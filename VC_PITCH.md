# 🚀 Project Titan: The Autonomous Technical Hiring Ecosystem

## 🌟 Executive Summary
**Project Titan** (POC) is an enterprise-grade, **Multi-Agent AI Interview Platform** designed to solve the "Engineering Bottleneck"—the inability of companies to scale technical hiring without sacrificing quality or burning out their best engineers. 

Unlike standard "AI wrappers" that act as simple chatbots, our platform leverages a **Cognitive Swarm Architecture** of 11 specialized AI agents to conduct deep, Socratic technical interviews, enforce genuine anti-cheating protocols, and deliver "Hire/No-Hire" verdicts with zero human latency.

---

## 🛑 The Problem
1.  **Engineering Drain**: Every hour a Senior Engineer spends interviewing is an hour lost on product development.
2.  **Signal Noise**: Traditional coding tests (LeetCode style) measure memorization, not adaptability or system thinking.
3.  **Integrity Crisis**: With the rise of GenAI, remote cheating has become undetectable by standard tools, rendering take-home assessments useless.
4.  **Bias & Inconsistency**: Human interviewers vary wildly in mood, criteria, and attention span.

---

## ⚡ The Solution: Cognitive Swarm Intelligence
We don't just "chat" with candidates; we **orchestrate** an interview.

Our **11-Agent Swarm** mimics a sophisticated hiring panel in real-time:
*   **The Orchestrator**: Manages round transitions and time.
*   **The Interrogator**: Asks tailored questions based on live responses.
*   **The Analytic Engine**: Detects semantic consistency and depth of knowledge.
*   **The Proctor (Focus Shield)**: Monitors behavioral cues, tab-switching, and browser events to flag integrity risks.
*   **The Architect**: Challenges the candidate on System Design and scalability (Round 3).

### 🛡️ Feature Deep Dive: The "Premium" Standard
The POC is functionally complete with a "High-Integrity" architecture:

#### 1. Real-World Simulation (Not Just Code)
*   **Round 1: Conceptual Depth**: Socratic dialogue that digs into "why," not just "what."
*   **Round 2: Algorithmic Proficiency**: Live Monaco Editor environment with real-time execution limits.
*   **Round 3: System Design**: Interactive architecture discussion often missed by automated tools.

#### 2. Military-Grade Integrity ("Focus Shield")
*   **One-Strike Policy**: Aggressive full-screen enforcement.
*   **Behavioral Telemetry**: Tracks mouse focus, blur events, and copy-paste velocity.
*   **Audit Logs**: Every infraction is time-stamped and severity-graded.

#### 3. Enterprise Resilience
*   **State Synchronization**: Interviews are crash-proof. Incremental saving ensures that even if the browser crashes, the candidate resumes exactly where they left off.
*   **Modular Reporting**: Instant generation of Executive Summaries, Plagiarism Dashboards, and Skill-Matrix Heatmaps.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack (The Engine)
*   **Core**: Next.js 14 (React Server Components), TypeScript
*   **Intelligence**: Google Gemini Pro + Hugging Face (Hybrid Inference)
*   **Swarm Logic**: Python-based Multi-Agent Orchestrator
*   **Data & State**: Supabase (PostgreSQL) + Firebase (Artifact Storage)
*   **Styling**: TailwindCSS (Modern, Responsive, Dark-Mode First)
